# Prism Topic Editor

## Goal

Create questions that are instantly understood, have two defensible choices, invite experience-rich comments, and do not depend on hate or misinformation.

## Topic checklist

- A and B are mutually understandable and similar in specificity.
- Neither side is framed as obviously smarter, kinder, richer, or more moral.
- Important assumptions are stated in one or two short sentences.
- The topic is answerable without expert knowledge.
- People can explain a personal reason rather than repeat a slogan.
- The topic can plausibly expose an overlooked trade-off.
- The title fits a mobile card.
- The subject is safe for an early community and advertiser-friendly environment.

## Avoid

Political candidates, gender-group generalization, religion or region superiority, unverified allegations about real people, high-stakes medical or legal decisions, minors in sexual contexts, self-harm, violence glorification, and false urgency.
